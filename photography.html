What's included Scope

What's included

  • A full-day or multi-day shoot on-site, scoped to the property's size
  • Every room type or unit type covered, not just a sample
  • Common areas, grounds, food, and amenities
  • Ground and aerial coverage from the same visit, not a separate booking
  • Edited, colour-graded final images, delivered as a private online gallery
  • Web-ready and print-ready exports, with usage rights for your own marketing

Scoped per property. A boutique villa and a 120-room resort aren't the same shoot, and the plan reflects that before a camera comes out.

Process Brief · Shoot · Edit · Deliver

How it runs

01
Brief & scope A short call or email thread to walk through the property, the room types, must-have shots, and any brand guidelines to match. Usually settled within a couple of days.
02
Shoot day, on-site Full-day or multi-day depending on the property. Ground and drone coverage happen in the same visit.
03
Edit & select Culled, graded, and delivered as a private gallery, typically within 5–7 business days of the shoot.
04
Delivery & usage Web and print-ready files, yours to use across your own site, listings, and ads.
