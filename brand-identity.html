What's included Scope

What's included

  • Logo and mark, with variations for different uses
  • Colour palette and typography, with the reasoning behind each choice
  • A practical brand guideline document, not just a mood board
  • Templates for the materials you'll actually reuse: signage, social, print, presentations, scoped to what you need
  • Ongoing brand media as a follow-on option, once the system exists

A new identity from the ground up and a refresh of what you've already got are different projects. The plan reflects which one this is before it starts.

Process Discover · Direct · Refine · Handoff

How it runs

01
Discovery The business, the audience, and what isn't working about the current identity, if there is one. Usually 3–5 days.
02
Concept & direction A small number of real directions, not ten thin options. Typically 1–2 weeks.
03
Refinement One direction, taken all the way, rather than five kept half-finished. About a week.
04
Guidelines & handoff The system documented so anyone on your team, or any vendor, can apply it correctly.
