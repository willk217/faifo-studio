02 — Featured project Case study · Auko, Phong Nha

Auko

Eco-wellness lodge · Phong Nha

Auko is a new eco-wellness lodge opening in Phong Nha, built around hydrotherapy, forest immersion and slow living. We were brought in to shoot the property from the ground and the air, and produce the films that show what the place actually feels like to be in.

  • Multi-day photo and video shoot across the property
  • Hero film and a set of shorter "way" films for different parts of the guest experience
  • Hundreds of photos covering rooms, treatments, food and grounds
  • Social-ready edits pulled straight from the shoot
  • Ongoing content aligned to their brand system and wellness calendar
